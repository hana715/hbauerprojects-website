04 · Bay Area · Claims & Advocacy

When the first fix is not a fix

A water-heater leak at a tenant-occupied residence. We directed emergency mitigation, opened the insurance claim and kept the tenants safe while work continued around them.

During quality review we found the flooring vendor had billed for a full replacement and installed a patch. We documented it, brought in independent inspectors, rejected the initial settlement and negotiated for the insurer to fund a complete redo with a new vendor.

Owner and insurer signed off at the final walk-through, and the claim was formally closed.
